# Demo narration

Spoken track for the hackathon demo (`bazaar-radar-demo.mp4`). Voice: Microsoft edge-tts `en-IN-NeerjaNeural` (Indian English), rate +18%, about 140 words a minute. 398 words. The picture is the local app in fixture mode. No API key is used and nothing is sent to SerpApi.

Small Indian online sellers guess festive demand and price before Diwali. Amazon.in has twenty lakh sellers, and sales open around the eighth of October. Bazaar Radar answers before that stock lands: a product idea, a target price, and the states you ship to. Fixture mode shows fixtures, zero credits. No API key is used.

SerpApi is the data layer. Google Trends gives the India curve, the five-year peak, states, and related queries. Amazon Search and Amazon Product give prices, ratings, sales buckets, and review insights. Google Shopping and Immersive Product show other stores. Ads Transparency counts India creatives. The Account API is the credit meter.

Rangoli colours, at one hundred and ninety rupees, for Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana. The five-year curve peaks in Diwali week. Last year’s peak started one day before Diwali. Momentum is one point one two times, flat.

Price bands, not a cheapest offer. From one hundred and ninety to two hundred and fifty rupees, sales-bucket share beats listing share, median four point zero. That is white space, on the target. Sponsored share is twenty percent. Review insights flag quality, colour selection, and value for money.

The card is deterministic Python, not a language model. Momentum at least one, a dense series, and white space on the target means GO. List in that band, push Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka, and fix those three complaints. These searches are fixtures, with zero live calls. Download Markdown for WhatsApp. Re-runs cost nothing.

Brass diya at six hundred and ninety-nine rupees is caution. Sparse Trends uses the head term diya: one point eight one times, rising, about eight days before Diwali. No white space: the band is crowded, near four stars. Sponsored share is n/a, not zero. Jaypore has forty-six India creatives. Complaints: size, heat resistance, and durability.

Diwali gift hamper is caution too. Trends is too thin to score, and fnp.com has two thousand India creatives, so ad pressure blocks a go.

On the twenty-seventh of September, brass toran at four hundred and ninety-nine rupees used fourteen searches. The cached re-run used zero credits. Guardrails cap a report at fourteen calls, a day at forty, and stop below twenty searches left.

Skip is falling demand on a crowded shelf. Caution is heavy ads, or no gap. Go is momentum plus white space. Trends is relative interest, not unit sales, and not a promise of profit.
